unfolding

/ʌnˈfəʊldɪŋ/ · noun

The gentle discovery of what is already within, allowing space for clarity, connection and new possibilities.

“Some things cannot be forced. They unfold.”

Welcome to Unfolding Portugal

This project is not a conventional retreat. It is a place to live and simply be, born from our own everyday lives and our wish to share calm, clarity and authenticity with others.

This is not an artificially created setting. It is our real home, opened to others.

What it is really about

The focus is not on becoming a better version of yourself, but on reconnecting with who you are. People come here at a turning point: between two chapters of life, after feeling depleted, during a time of change or uncertainty or simply because they sense that they no longer want to carry on as before.

This space is here to help you find your way back to yourself. Simply, clearly and without pressure.

A natural rhythm

Your stay does not follow a rigid structure, but it has a gentle underlying flow:

Arriving. Slowly settling into the space, getting to know one another, finding your bearings and taking that first deep breath.

Slowing down. Giving your nervous system time to settle, finding your own rhythm, with fewer distractions and more quiet.

Opening up. First conversations, gentle invitations and a growing connection with the group and with yourself.

Going deeper. Space for deeper exploration and breathwork, allowing emotions to surface and move.

Integration. Making sense of what has emerged, understanding it and finding its place in your life. Rest becomes increasingly important.

Finding a new direction. Room for clarity, fresh thoughts and possible next steps to emerge.

A mindful departure. Returning to everyday life with intention. Not an abrupt ending, but a gradual transition.

The space itself

Guests stay in their own simple living spaces—caravans nestled in nature. The emphasis is on calm, simplicity and taking responsibility for your own needs.

There is also a shared space: sheltered, gentle and supportive. A space that feels like an embrace—a roof over your head, a place to feel safe and held.

Embrace the Experience

We offer gentle impulses

  1. 1

    Breathwork

    Explore breathwork for different moments in your day: morning practices to gently awaken, energising breathwork during the day and calming evening sessions to quiet the mind and unwind.

  2. 2

    Meditation

    Twice a week, an experienced meditation facilitator joins us for an hour of guided practice. These gentle sessions offer space to slow down, settle into the present moment and explore a sense of inner stillness.

  3. 3

    Bodywork

    Twice a week, we are warmly welcomed at a beautiful, well-known yoga retreat nearby to practise in its dedicated yoga dome. A special setting to explore mindful movement, connect with your breath and reconnect with your body.

  4. 4

    Coaching Conversations

    Individual coaching conversations, offered according to your needs, with personal support and space for what matters to you.

  5. 5

    Creative Space

    Painting, writing, journaling and creative expression—gentle invitations to explore what is within and give it room to take shape.

  6. 6

    Time in Nature

    Beach walks and time outdoors, alone or together. Space to breathe, take in your surroundings and enjoy a slower pace.

  7. 7

    Cooking Together

    Preparing simple meals together and sharing the table. An everyday way to connect, contribute and enjoy being with one another.

  8. 8

    Free Space

    Not everything is planned. There is room for whatever unfolds: resting in a hammock, cuddling the cats and dogs who live here with us or retreating to your own space.

  9. 9

    An Optional Surf Moment

    For those who feel drawn to it, a surf moment towards the end of your stay offers a playful way to recognise your inner progress and celebrate how far you have come—perhaps by catching a wave of your own.

Everyone moves at their own pace.

Explore Your Journey with Us

Journaling and creative expression. Time for yourself and your own inner process. Beautiful walks by the ocean. Cooking and sharing meals together. Time for connection, conversation and genuine togetherness—and, above all, plenty of space to simply be.

No packed schedule. No “you have to.” Instead, a small and personal setting where you can slow down enough to hear what is asking for your attention right now.

This space is not a substitute for therapy, an intensive intervention or a tightly scheduled retreat. It is a place to live, with guidance and support. Personal responsibility is central. At the same time, there is steadiness, structure and thoughtful personal support.

What emerges here is a space that holds you. A place where you can begin to sense again:

Who am I right now? What do I really need? And how do I want to move forward?

It is not a fixed concept. It is a living, evolving space—and that is precisely its strength.

If you are longing for a pause, a change of perspective or simply a little more sunshine, nature and connection, feel free to reach out.

About Me

Antonia

Founder & Host

I’m 50, a proud mother of two wonderful children, and have called Portugal home for almost 30 years. With roots in hospitality and a lifelong love of creativity, I enjoy building and shaping things, turning what is already there into something new, meaningful and beautiful.

My life is colourful, curious and always in motion. Personal growth is my greatest passion: discovering what becomes possible when we open ourselves to change, new perspectives and new beginnings.

I believe some of the most beautiful things in life emerge when we work with what we have and openly welcome what comes our way. I founded Unfolding Portugal, a project born from that very spirit.

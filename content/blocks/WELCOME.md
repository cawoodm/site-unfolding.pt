## Welcome to Unfolding Portugal!

This project is not a conventional retreat. It is a place to live and simply be, born from our own everyday lives and our wish to share calm, clarity and authenticity with others.

This is not an artificially created setting. It is our real home, opened to others.

## What it is really about

The focus is not on becoming a better version of yourself, but on reconnecting with who you are. People come here at a turning point: between two chapters of life, after feeling depleted, during a time of change or uncertainty or simply because they sense that they no longer want to carry on as before.

This space is here to help you find your way back to yourself. Simply, clearly and without pressure.

## A natural rhythm

Your stay does not follow a rigid structure, but it has a gentle underlying flow:

**Arriving.** Slowly settling into the space, getting to know one another, finding your bearings and taking that first deep breath.

**Slowing down.** Giving your nervous system time to settle, finding your own rhythm, with fewer distractions and more quiet.

**Opening up.** First conversations, gentle invitations and a growing connection with the group and with yourself.

**Going deeper.** Space for deeper exploration and breathwork, allowing emotions to surface and move.

**Integration.** Making sense of what has emerged, understanding it and finding its place in your life. Rest becomes increasingly important.

**Finding a new direction.** Room for clarity, fresh thoughts and possible next steps to emerge.

**A mindful departure.** Returning to everyday life with intention. Not an abrupt ending, but a gradual transition.

## The space itself

Guests stay in their own simple living spaces—caravans nestled in nature. The emphasis is on calm, simplicity and taking responsibility for your own needs.

There is also a shared space: sheltered, gentle and supportive. A space that feels like an embrace—a roof over your head, a place to feel safe and held.
